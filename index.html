What we run

Four walls, one crew

01

Graffiti & Mural

Cans, caps, and sketchbooks. Weekly sessions on legal walls, plus letterform and character workshops.

02

Breaking & Street Dance

Footwork, power moves, and cyphers. Open floor practice every week, battle prep before events.

03

Freestyle & Spoken Word

Cyphers, open mic nights, and writing sessions for anyone who wants to put bars or verses together.

04

Live Art & Events

Campus fests, live-paint battles, and pop-up walls — where all four disciplines share one stage.

Why join

Made by students who couldn't find a wall

Street Arts Club started as a handful of people practicing in a parking lot. Now it's a full crew across four disciplines, open to any student — first-timers included. Bring your own style, we'll bring the space.
